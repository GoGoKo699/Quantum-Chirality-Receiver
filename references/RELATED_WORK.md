# Related work

The experiment asks for a single handedness bit from three product qubits arriving in a fixed order. One calibrated unital qubit channel is available across the first arrival gap. The receiver reaches the exact worst-case discrimination score using only a classical record across the second gap. The upper bound permits arbitrary early instruments and coherent access to both later registers. The bibliography below locates the ingredients behind the measurement and the closest precedents for that resource comparison.

## Relational information

For single-qubit density operators with Bloch vectors $`r_1,r_2,r_3`$, the imaginary part of the ordered third-order Bargmann invariant is the signed volume divided by four:

```math
4\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Tr}(\rho_1\rho_2\rho_3)
=\det[r_1\;r_2\;r_3].
```

Oszmaniec, Brod and Galvão give the general cycle-test route to complex Bargmann invariants for pure and mixed states. The handedness receiver reads the sign of this same imaginary part from three consecutive specimens. Simonov, Wagner and Galvão use partial classical descriptions to evaluate longer invariants with fewer supplied states. Those descriptions provide an additional resource for their measurement design. Li, Wagner and Zhang show that pair overlaps suffice to test coplanarity. Triples with the same pairwise Gram matrix can have the same nonzero volume magnitude and opposite signs, so distinguishing the sign requires information beyond these overlaps. [Oszmaniec et al., New J. Phys. 26, 013053 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ad1a27) · [Simonov et al., Phys. Rev. A 112, 062435 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/mrpz-xq28) · [Li et al., Phys. Rev. A 113, 012428 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/tpgw-v6ht)

Collins et al. encode spatial parity in rotationally invariant physical states. Here the label is the orientation of three ordered Bloch vectors under common proper rotations in a Pauli frame. The distinction matters for physical implementations in which the Bloch components are axial spin coordinates. [Phys. Rev. A 72, 022304 (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.72.022304)

## Related portfolio results

- **Vacancy Chirality Readout** provides the fully separable effect bound and the uniform six-setting Pauli measurement used by the classical receiver. Its main result is a precision separation for estimating chirality from repeated copies in a four-mode, site-parity detection architecture. [Separable benchmark](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Vacancy-Chirality-Readout/blob/6b22b2a06c0c8adc53d69765fa907ebb8a6d33ef/research/SEPARABLE_BENCHMARK.md) · [Parity theory](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Vacancy-Chirality-Readout/blob/6b22b2a06c0c8adc53d69765fa907ebb8a6d33ef/research/COMPLETE_PARITY_THEORY.md)
- **Quantum Blind Entanglement Purification** uses the chiral multiplicity sectors as filters and retains a quantum output at the successful branches. The handedness protocol ends with a classical decision. [Chirality in purification](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Blind-Entanglement-Purification/blob/461b9b9651cc370387dfe691619fac6435da985d/science/chirality.md)
- **Quantum Holonomy Sampling** estimates a Bargmann phase for four or more labeled sources. Its coherent-run budget limits source copies per run. This receiver charges one use of storage at a specified cut in a three-specimen experiment. [Model](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Holonomy-Sampling/blob/1c24e1e34984a1ea1318906025a1fe526108fdec/science/model.md)
- **Quantum Streaming Memory** also tracks storage across time cuts. Its memory law addresses recovery of erased quantum payloads at a fixed deadline and with delayed acknowledgments. [Model](https://github.com/GoGoKo699/Quantum-Streaming-Memory/blob/f13d93a1308ff3c9868e8a64e34da2f7a3904112/science/model.md)
