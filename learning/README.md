# Learn the receiver

Start with three qubits, each prepared once and delivered in sequence. Their Bloch vectors form an ordered triple. We want the sign of its oriented volume from a single triplet, in a lab whose axes need not match the source's. The route follows what can be learned at each arrival and what survives between arrivals.

The milestones move from one-qubit states and measurements through the two temporal gaps, then join the attainable receiver and the bound on all receiver strategies.
